GrIMSON Mk-I prEMIUM LINE

Handcrafted elegance. Built for real work.

Handcrafted Performance for Field and Collection

The MK-I PREMIUM LINE represents the handcrafted expression of the GRIMSON platform, combining reinforced tanto geometry with genuine Damascus steel. Available in both Raindrop Damascus and Twisted Damascus patterns, each knife is individually finished using carefully selected premium handle materials.

Refined in appearance but uncompromising in performance, the PREMIUM LINE is designed for users who appreciate both functionality and craftsmanship. Premium natural materials, Damascus steel, and carefully finished details create a knife equally suited for demanding field use or as the centerpiece of a serious collection. Produced in limited numbers, every MK-I PREMIUM reflects the philosophy that quality should never be mass-produced.

Specifications

  • Blade Steel: Damascus steel — 15N20 and 1095 carbon steel
  • Hardness: ~57–59 HRC
  • Blade Length: ~140 mm
  • Blade Width: 34 mm
  • Spine Thickness: 5 mm
  • Grind: Flat grind
  • Edge Geometry: Reinforced tanto
  • Handle: Premium pakka / rosewood handle scales
  • Handle Length: ~120 mm
  • Overall Length: ~270 mm
  • Knife Weight: ~225–230 g
  • Sheath: Brown or black leather sheath
  • Carry System: Belt carry leather sheath
  • Sheath Weight: ~120 g
  • Total Weight (Knife + Sheath): ~348 g

Built for collectors, outdoor enthusiasts, and professionals who appreciate traditional craftsmanship, the GRIMSON MK-I PREMIUM LINE combines Damascus steel, premium natural handle materials, reinforced tanto geometry, and practical field-ready construction. Produced in limited numbers, it represents the highest expression of the GRIMSON platform.
