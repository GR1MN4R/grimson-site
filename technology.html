Design Philosophy

GRIMSON tools are built on a simple principle: structure defines performance. Every line, angle, and proportion is determined by how the tool behaves under real use.

The design process prioritizes durability, control, and reliability over aesthetic excess. Each component is refined to perform consistently under load, rather than optimized for appearance.

Geometry, material selection, and balance are treated as a unified system. The result is a tool that remains stable, predictable, and effective across a wide range of applications.

No feature exists without purpose. Every detail contributes directly to function.

Core Engineering Elements

Reinforced Geometry — the tanto profile strengthens the tip structure, increasing resistance to deformation and failure under pressure.
Structural Spine — a 4 mm spine thickness provides rigidity and load distribution, supporting stability during demanding tasks.
Controlled Edge Profile — the edge geometry is configured for durability and stability, prioritizing edge retention over excessive sharpness.
Material Selection — 440B stainless steel is selected for corrosion resistance, structural durability, and practical field maintenance.
Functional Balance — weight distribution and handle design ensure controlled handling and reduced fatigue during extended use.
Grip Integrity — the glass-fiber reinforced polymer handle provides structural stability and consistent grip under varying conditions.
