GRIMSON MAINTENANCE

KEEP YOUR EDGE.

GENERAL CARE

Clean the blade after use with a soft, damp cloth and dry it thoroughly before storage. Do not leave the knife wet or exposed to salt, chemicals, acids or other corrosive substances.

CORE LINE — 440B STAINLESS STEEL

440B stainless steel provides strong corrosion resistance, but it is not corrosion-proof. After exposure to moisture, salt water or aggressive materials, clean and completely dry the blade.

A thin coat of suitable knife or mineral oil (e.g. food-grade mineral oil or liquid paraffin) may be applied for additional protection during extended storage.

PREMIUM LINE — DAMASCUS STEEL

GRIMSON Premium blades use 15N20 and 1095 Damascus steel and require more attentive care. Always clean and thoroughly dry the blade immediately after use.

Apply a very thin protective layer of suitable knife or mineral oil (e.g. food-grade mineral oil, liquid paraffin or camellia knife oil) before storage.

Never store a Damascus blade while damp.

HANDLE & FASTENERS

Periodically inspect the handle fasteners. If necessary, carefully tighten loose screws using the correct tool. Do not overtighten.

To check the fit, hold the knife sideways toward a strong light source and observe the joint between the handle scales and the tang. Tighten only until no light is visible through the joint, then stop.

Clean handle surfaces with a soft cloth and avoid prolonged exposure to aggressive chemicals.

SHEATH & STORAGE

Never store a wet or dirty knife inside its sheath. Allow both the knife and sheath to dry completely before storage.

For long-term storage, we recommend keeping the knife clean, dry and protected outside the sheath.

SHARPENING

Maintain the original blade geometry when sharpening. Remove only as much material as necessary to restore the edge. Improper sharpening or excessive material removal can permanently alter the blade geometry.

For routine sharpening, approximately 600 grit may be used for initial edge restoration, followed by approximately 1000 grit for refining the edge. If the edge only requires light maintenance, sharpening may begin directly with the finer grit.

Always sharpen both sides evenly and maintain the existing factory edge angle.

IMPORTANT

Do not use the knife as a pry bar, screwdriver, chisel or striking tool. Avoid excessive lateral loading of the blade and direct contact with live electrical systems.
