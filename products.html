GrIMSON Mk-I

Reinforced tanto engineered for strength and control

Intended Applications: Construction and Field Use

The MK-I is GRIMSON’s flagship launch model, built around the strength and control of reinforced tanto blade geometry. Designed for demanding utility work, the blade’s angular tip provides exceptional penetration strength and structural durability where conventional profiles often fail.

Clean lines, durable steel, and a controlled working profile make the MK-I a dependable field tool rather than a decorative piece. Its balanced proportions and reinforced construction are engineered for real tasks, where reliability, precision, and repeatable performance matter more than excess.

Specifications

  • Blade Steel: 440B stainless steel
  • Hardness: 58–60 HRC
  • Blade Length: ~140 mm
  • Blade Width: 34 mm
  • Spine Thickness: 4 mm
  • Grind: Flat grind
  • Edge Geometry: Reinforced tanto
  • Handle: Glass-fiber reinforced polymer (GFRP)
  • Handle Length: ~120 mm
  • Overall Length: ~270 mm
  • Knife Weight: 286 g
  • Sheath: Reinforced polymer sheath
  • Carry System: Integrated quick-release belt mount
  • Sheath Weight: 125 g
  • Total Weight (Knife + Sheath): ~411 g

Built as a professional utility knife for construction and field work, the GRIMSON MK-I is suitable for controlled cutting tasks involving packaging, plastic sheeting, gypsum board, insulation materials, and general worksite use where strength and durability are essential.
